Overview

Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos & Thailand — in one journey.

Southeast Asia is home to the vibrant nations of Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos and Thailand. This comprehensive and far-flung journey reveals their historical, cultural and culinary treasures, from karst-adorned Ha Long Bay and incomparable Angkor Wat to charming Luang Prabang and temple-laden Bangkok.

Journey Highlights

What awaits you.

  • Engage with the complex history of Vietnam, north and south, visiting the infamous "Hanoi Hilton" and inspecting the Cu Chi Tunnels, where you may meet a Vietcong veteran.
  • Glide among stunning limestone islands and watch the sun set and rise again during a relaxing overnight cruise on tranquil Ha Long Bay.
  • Venture into the heart of daily life in Ho Chi Minh City, riding by cyclo through the streets at dawn, stopping for coffee and savoring sensational pho at a culinary hotspot.
  • Explore Cambodia's most storied temples with an expert local guide — including Angkor Wat, Bayon and Ta Prohm — during a two-night sojourn in Siem Reap.
  • In serene Luang Prabang, meet with locals in their home for a baci ceremony and take part in an alms-giving tradition that immerses you in the community's spiritual life.

Where You'll Stay

Five destinations, five landmark stays.

Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi

A French colonial landmark in the heart of Hanoi, hosting ambassadors, writers and statesmen for more than 100 years. 358 rooms and suites across the historic Heritage Wing and neo-classical Opera Wing, three restaurants, three bars, and a spa, sauna and fitness center.

Lyra Grandeur

A stylish modern superyacht cruising Ha Long and Lan Ha Bays. 33 cabins each with en-suite bath, panoramic views and private balconies; onboard restaurant, bar, plunge pool, Jacuzzi and waterslide.

The Reverie Saigon

Perched atop the Times Square Building in District 1. 286 eclectic rooms and suites with Italian and Vietnamese touches, five restaurants, a pool, fitness center, salon and spa.

Raffles Grand Hotel d'Angkor

A historic fixture of Siem Reap's French Quarter since 1932, set among 15 acres of gardens five miles from Angkor Wat. 119 Art Deco rooms and suites, five restaurants and bars, and a spa with sauna and steam room.
